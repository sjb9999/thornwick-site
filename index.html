Thornwick: Garden Merge
A cosy story merge game by Littlehollow Games

Help and support

Wren, a gardener, takes on the overgrown walled garden behind Thornwick Hall to save the village fair. Merge seeds, tools and treasures on the board to serve the villagers, then spend what you earn restoring the garden, one patch at a time, and find out what's been buried there all these years.

Questions and problems

Stuck, found a bug, or just want to say hello? Email fernhollowgame@gmail.com. Telling us your phone model helps.

Common questions

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress? Your progress is saved on your phone and comes across when you restore your new phone from a backup of the old one.

How do I turn the reminders off? In the game, open the Menu and switch off Alerts. You can also turn them off in your phone's Settings, under Notifications.

How do I see how to play again? Open the Menu and tap How to play.

I've run out of tea. Your flask fills up again by itself over time, a sip at a time. You can also fill it straight away with Gold Leaf.

Privacy

No accounts, and your progress stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
